### Drill 2 – Coffee Shop Receipt System

### Scenario

A coffee shop records a customer's order and computes the total payment.

If the total purchase reaches the required amount, the customer becomes eligible for a free cookie.

### Concepts Used

- Variables
- Arithmetic Operators
- Conditional Statements
- Console Output

### Sample Output


=== Coffee Shop Receipt ===

Customer: Kyle
Drink: Iced Coffee
Quantity: 2
Price per Cup: ₱120
Total Amount: ₱240

Status: Eligible for a free cookie!
